Use Mercur to let each store manage and sell its own products.

The Catalog domain holds products, variants, categories, collections, tags, and
types. Every product belongs to the store that created it through the
`product_seller` link, and the store sells it directly: price and stock live on
the product's variants. Submissions follow a status lifecycle before they go
live.

<Note>
  **Catalog = Medusa's Product module + Mercur's marketplace layer.** Products,
  variants, categories, and collections are Medusa's native `Product` module.
  Mercur adds the `product_seller` link (which store a product belongs to), the
  `draft` → `proposed` → `published` / `rejected` status lifecycle, and its
  own product workflows. The store's SKU, price, and stock live on the
  product's variants.
</Note>

## Key features

- **Store-owned products:** a store creates its products and only that store can see, edit, or delete them.
- **Product–seller link:** the `product_seller` link records the one store a product belongs to.
- **Approval governance:** a `draft` → `proposed` → `published` / `rejected` lifecycle, with vendor-created products defaulting to `proposed`.
- **Audit trail:** every submission and edit is recorded in the product-change pipeline.
- **Native Medusa structure:** variants, categories, collections, tags, and types come straight from Medusa's Product module.
- **Sold through variants:** the variant carries the SKU, price, and inventory; a cart line item points at the variant.
