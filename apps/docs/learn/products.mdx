title: "Products"

---

Every product belongs to the **store that created it**. The store sells the product directly: the SKU, price, and stock live on the product's own variants, and a cart line item points at a variant.

## Ownership

Ownership is recorded in two places:

| Mechanism | What it controls |
|-----------|-----------------|
| `product_seller` link | The one store the product belongs to. Vendor routes only return, edit, or delete products linked to the authenticated store; any other product id answers `404`. The order split takes the line item's store from this link. |
| Creator attribution | The product's [change history](/rc/learn/product-requests) records who created and changed it, for audit. |

The link is created for the submitting store when the product is created. A product must have exactly one store: a product without a store cannot be checked out.

## Status lifecycle

```
DRAFT ──> PROPOSED ──> PUBLISHED
              │
              └──────> REJECTED
```

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `draft` | Work in progress, not submitted |
| `proposed` | Submitted for review — visible only to its creator until published |
| `published` | Live on the storefront |
| `rejected` | Declined by the operator |

Vendor-created products default to **`proposed`**. The operator reviews the submission and publishes or rejects it — see [Product Requests & Approvals](/rc/learn/product-requests).

## Visibility rules

| Surface | What is visible |
|---------|----------------|
| Vendor | Only the store's own products (any status) |
| Admin | Every product, regardless of status or store |
| Store | Published products of visible stores |

## Editing products

A store edits only its own products — updating details, adding or removing variants, changing attributes. Edits flow through the [change-request pipeline](/rc/learn/product-requests), so every change is recorded and, when product requests are enabled, reviewed by the operator first.

## Price, stock, and shipping

The product's variants carry the store's SKU, prices, and inventory items, and the product uses one of the store's own shipping profiles. There is no separate listing entity between the product and the store.
